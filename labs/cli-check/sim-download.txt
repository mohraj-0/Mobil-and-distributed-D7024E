BLUEBERRY PIE
Version: 1
Change: Initial blueberry pie recipe.

Ingredients:
- 500 g blueberries
- 150 g sugar
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 300 g flour
- 150 g cold butter
- 5 tablespoons cold water

Method:
1. Rub the butter into the flour, then mix in the cold water to form dough.
2. Roll out the dough.
3. Line a pie dish with half the dough. Reserve the rest for the top.
4. Mix blueberries, sugar and cornstarch. Spoon the filling into the pie dish.
5. Cover with the remaining dough and cut small steam vents.
6. Leave the top crust unglazed.
7. Bake in a preheated oven at 180 C for 40 minutes, until golden and bubbling.
8. Let the pie cool before serving.
